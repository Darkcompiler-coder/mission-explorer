How to use this guide

Check the status and the context

Mission status can change, and different sources may describe a spacecraft as “inactive,” “silent” or “mission complete.” This site uses those terms carefully: passive devices can still support science, a lander can remain on a surface after its mission ends, and a probe that continues to travel may no longer be communicating.

NASA, JPL and partner agencies are credited as the sources of mission facts. This site is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
